# 中1英語 Unit 3「疑問詞」まとめの問題

<p class="lead">Unit 3-1「疑問詞①」と Unit 3-2「疑問詞②」の総まとめです。目標時間 40分。8割とれたら Unit 4 へ進みましょう。</p>

<div class="memo" markdown="1">

**チェックリスト**　□ 疑問詞＋ふつうの疑問文の語順で書ける　□ who が主語の文（Who plays 〜? ― Ken does.）
□ what＋名詞・what time・whose＋名詞・which A or B　□ where・when・why（Because）・how（様子・手段）
□ how many＋複数名詞・how much・how old・how long・how tall　□ 答えを見て、質問の疑問詞がわかる

</div>

## まとめの問題

#### <span class="no">1</span> 対話が成り立つように、（　）に適する語を1語ずつ入れなさい。

<div class="cols2" markdown="1">

(1) (　　　) is that girl? ― She's Aya.
(2) (　　　) is your house? ― It's near the park.
(3) (　　　) umbrella is this? ― It's mine.
(4) (　　　) do you go to school? ― I walk.
(5) (　　　) do you like spring? ― Because I like flowers.
(6) (　　　) is the concert? ― It's next Sunday.
(7) What (　　　) is it? ― It's five o'clock.
(8) How (　　　) CDs do you have? ― About fifty.
(9) How (　　　) is your cat? ― It's two years old.
(10) Who (　　　) the dishes? ― My brother does.（wash を適する形に）

</div>

#### <span class="no">2</span> 下線部をたずねる疑問文を作りなさい。

(1) My sister likes <u>cats</u>.
(2) <u>Mr. Hayashi</u> teaches music.
(3) I have <u>two</u> bikes.
(4) This is <u>Yumi's</u> racket.
(5) Kenji lives <u>in Odawara</u>.
(6) This cap is <u>1,800 yen</u>.

#### <span class="no">3</span> 次の質問に対する答えとして最も適するものを、1〜4の中から1つずつ選びなさい。

(1) Which is your bag, the black one or the white one?
　1．Yes, it is.　2．The white one is.　3．It's a bag.　4．I have a black one.
(2) How tall is your father?
　1．He's forty.　2．He's 180 centimeters tall.　3．He's fine.　4．He's very kind.
(3) Who usually cooks dinner in your family?
　1．My mother is.　2．My mother does.　3．Yes, she does.　4．At six.
(4) How long do you sleep every night?
　1．About eight hours.　2．At eleven.　3．It's long.　4．In my room.

(2) Where does your aunt lives?
(3) Who does teach science?
(4) How many child does Mr. Ono have?

#### <span class="no">5</span> 日本語の意味になるように（　）内の語句を並べかえなさい。ただし、それぞれ**使わない語が1つ**あります。

(1) あなたは何時に夕食を食べますか。
　( time / what / when / do / you / eat ) dinner?
(2) あなたのお母さんはどこで働いていますか。
　( where / does / is / your mother / work )?
(3) この本はいくらですか。
　( much / many / is / how / this book )?

#### <span class="no">7</span> 次の対話の（　）に入る最も適するものを、1〜4の中から1つずつ選びなさい。

(1) A: (　　)
　B: It's Thursday. We have P.E. today.
　1．What time is it?　2．What day is it today?　3．When is P.E.?　4．How is the weather?
(2) A: (　　)
　B: By train. It takes about thirty minutes.
　1．Where do you live?　2．Why do you take the train?　3．How do you come here?　4．What do you take?

#### <span class="no">8</span> 次の英文は、中学生のハルカ（Haruka）が、新しいALTのグリーン先生（Ms. Green）にインタビューした内容です。これを読んで、あとの問いに答えなさい。

<div class="ex" markdown="1">

Haruka: Where are you from, Ms. Green?
Ms. Green: I'm from New Zealand.
Haruka: What do you like about Japan?
Ms. Green: I like Japanese food. I eat sushi every week.
Haruka: Why do you like sushi?
Ms. Green: Because the fish in Japan is very fresh.
Haruka: How many brothers and sisters do you have?
Ms. Green: I have one sister. She lives in Wellington. She is a vet. She takes care of animals.
Haruka: Which do you like, dogs or cats?
Ms. Green: I like dogs. I have a dog in New Zealand. He is with my sister now.

</div>

※ fresh（新鮮な）　Wellington（ウェリントン：ニュージーランドの都市）　vet（獣医）　take care of 〜（〜の世話をする）

(1) Where is Ms. Green from? に英語で答えなさい。
(2) How many sisters does Ms. Green have? に英語で答えなさい。
(3) 本文の内容に合うものとして最も適するものを、1〜4の中から1つ選びなさい。
　1．Ms. Green eats sushi every day.
　2．Ms. Green's sister is a teacher in Wellington.
　3．Ms. Green likes sushi because the fish in Japan is fresh.
　4．Ms. Green likes cats, and she has a cat.
(4) 次の質問の答えになるように、（　）に適する1語を入れなさい。
　Q: Who is with Ms. Green's dog now?　A: Her (　　　) is.

- 2つの文で**ちがう疑問詞**を使うこと。そのうち1つは **how** を使うこと。

## 解答・解説

<div class="kaito" markdown="1">

#### <span class="no">1</span>
(1) Who　(2) Where　(3) Whose　(4) How　(5) Why　(6) When　(7) time　(8) many　(9) old　(10) washes
**解説**　(4) 「歩いて行く」と手段を答えているので how。(10) who が主語で、答えが My brother **does** なので、三人称単数の washes。

#### <span class="no">2</span>
(1) What does your sister like?
(2) Who teaches music?
(3) How many bikes do you have?
(4) Whose racket is this?
(5) Where does Kenji live?
(6) How much is this cap?
**解説**　(2) 主語をたずねるので Mr. Hayashi を Who に置きかえるだけ（does は使わない）。(3)(4) many / whose は名詞とセットで文頭へ。

#### <span class="no">3</span>
(1) 2　(2) 2　(3) 2　(4) 1
**解説**　(3) Who cooks 〜? には〈人＋does〉。(4) 時間の長さなので About eight hours.。2 は「何時に」の答え。

#### <span class="no">4</span>
(1) What sport **do you** play?
(2) Where does your aunt **live**?
(3) Who **teaches** science?
(4) How many **children** does Mr. Ono have?
(5) Whose is this notebook? ― It's **hers**.

#### <span class="no">5</span>
(1) What time do you eat dinner?　使わない語：**when**
(2) Where does your mother work?　使わない語：**is**
(3) How much is this book?　使わない語：**many**
**解説**　(1) 「何時に」は what time。when も「いつ」だが、ここでは time が使われるので when はあまる。(3) 値段は how much。

#### <span class="no">6</span>
(1) Whose bike is that?
(2) Which do you like, summer or winter?
(3) How long do you watch TV every day?
(4) Why does Ken get up early?

#### <span class="no">7</span>
(1) 2　(2) 3
**解説**　(1) Thursday（曜日）を答えているので What day。(2) By train は手段なので how。

#### <span class="no">8</span>
(1) She is (She's) from New Zealand.
(2) She has one (sister).
(3) 3
(4) sister
**解説**　(3) 1 every day ではなく every week。2 姉妹は獣医（vet）。4 好きなのは犬。(4) He is with my sister now. の He は犬のこと。

#### <span class="no">9</span>
（解答例）How old are you? ／ What Japanese food do you like?
（ほかの例）How do you come to school? ／ When is your birthday? ／ Who is your favorite singer?
**解説**　採点のポイントは ①疑問詞が2つともちがう、②1つは how を使っている、③疑問詞のあとが疑問文の語順になっている。初対面の先生に How old are you? はやや失礼なこともあるので、実際には How do you come to school? などのほうがおすすめ。
