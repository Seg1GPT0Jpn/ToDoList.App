# 中3英語 Unit 16「間接疑問文と不定詞の発展」まとめの問題

<p class="lead">Unit 16-1「間接疑問文」と Unit 16-2「不定詞の発展」の総まとめです。目標時間 45分。8割とれたら Unit 17 へ進みましょう。</p>

<div class="memo" markdown="1">

**チェックリスト**　□ 間接疑問文は「疑問詞＋**主語＋動詞**」の語順（do / does / did は消して動詞の形を変える）　□ who が主語のときは「who＋動詞」のまま
□ It is 〜 (for 人) to ― の It は形式主語（文型は SVC）　□ too 〜 to ― ＝ so 〜 that … can't ―（時制と目的語 it に注意）
□ want / ask / tell ＋人＋to 〜（O が to 以下をする）・not to 〜　□ how / what / where to 〜 ＝ 疑問詞＋主語＋should 〜
□ let / make / help ＋O＋原形（SVOC：O が C する）

</div>

## まとめの問題

#### <span class="no">1</span> （　）内から適するものを選びなさい。

<div class="cols2" markdown="1">

(1) Do you know where ( does he live ・ he lives )?
(2) I don't know what ( this is ・ is this ).
(3) Can you tell me who ( did write ・ wrote ) this letter?
(4) It is important ( of ・ for ) us to save water.
(5) My teacher told me ( speak ・ to speak ) louder.
(6) Let me ( help ・ to help ) you.
(7) This box is too big ( to carry ・ carrying ).
(8) I'll show you how ( make ・ to make ) a paper crane.

</div>

#### <span class="no">2</span> 2つの文を、間接疑問文を使って1つの文にしなさい。

(1) I know. + What does she want?
(2) Do you know? + When is his birthday?
(3) Please tell me. + Where did you buy the bag?
(4) I don't know. + Who broke the window?
(5) I wonder. + How many students are there in the gym?

#### <span class="no">3</span> 2つの文がほぼ同じ意味になるように、（　）に適する語を1語ずつ入れなさい。

(1) Studying science is interesting for me.
　(　　　) is interesting for me (　　　) study science.
(2) She was so busy that she couldn't eat lunch.
　She was (　　　) busy (　　　) eat lunch.
(3) Tell me what I should bring.
　Tell me (　　　) (　　　) bring.
(4) My mother said to me, "Please go shopping."
　My mother (　　　) me (　　　) go shopping.
(5) My father said, "You can use my bike." I was happy.
　My father (　　　) me (　　　) his bike.

#### <span class="no">4</span> 次の英文にはまちがいが1か所ずつあります。正しい英文に直しなさい。

<div class="cols2" markdown="1">

(1) I don't know where is the station.
(2) Do you know what time did he come?
(3) I want him come to the party.
(4) The movie made me to cry.

</div>

#### <span class="no">5</span> 日本語の意味になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえなさい。ただし、それぞれ**使わない語が1つ**あります。

(1) あなたはあの女の人がだれか知っていますか。
　Do you know ( is / who / that woman / does )?
(2) 私たちにとって、毎朝朝食を食べることは大切だ。
　( to / it / us / is / for / of / important ) eat breakfast every morning.
(3) 先生は私たちに、教室で走らないように言った。
　Our teacher ( not / us / told / to / don't / run ) in the classroom.
(4) 私は何を言えばよいかわからなかった。
　I didn't ( say / know / what / to / how ).

#### <span class="no">6</span> 次の日本語を英語にしなさい。

(1) 私は彼がなぜ遅れたのか知りません。
(2) 私はあなたに私の町を訪れてほしい。
(3) このコンピュータの使い方を教えてくれますか。（how を使って）
(4) 母は私に夕食を作らせた。（make を使って）

#### <span class="no">7</span> 次の対話の（　）に入る最も適するものを、それぞれ1〜4の中から1つずつ選び、その番号を答えなさい。

(1) A: Excuse me. Could you tell me (　　)?
　B: Sure. It's on the third floor.
　1．where the music room is　2．where is the music room　3．where does the music room　4．the music room where is
(2) A: I'm going to visit Kyoto, but I can't decide (　　) first.
　B: How about Kiyomizu-dera?
　1．where should go　2．where to visit　3．what to go　4．to where visit
(3) A: Your English is very good!
　B: Thank you. My aunt in Canada helped me (　　) it.
　1．study　2．studied　3．studies　4．studying

#### <span class="no">8</span> 次の英文は、中学生のリク（Riku）が英語の授業で行ったスピーチです。これを読んで、あとの問いに答えなさい。

<div class="ex" markdown="1">

Last summer, I joined a volunteer program at a library. On the first day, a librarian asked me to read picture books to small children. I didn't know how to read them well, so I was nervous. A girl in front of me asked, "Why is the rabbit crying?" I didn't know what to say. Then the librarian told me to ask the children what they thought. When I asked them, they began to share their ideas. Their smiles made me feel relaxed.
Through this experience, I learned ①**it is important to listen to others**. Now I want many students to join this program. Do you know when the next program starts? It starts in August.

</div>

(1) What did the librarian ask Riku to do on the first day? 英語で答えなさい。
(2) 下線部①を日本語にしなさい。
(3) 本文中から、間接疑問文を1つ探し、そのまま書きぬきなさい。
(4) 本文の内容に合うものとして最も適するものを、1〜4の中から1つ選び、その番号を答えなさい。
　1．Riku knew how to read picture books well before the program.
　2．The librarian told Riku to answer the girl's question by himself.
　3．Riku felt relaxed because the children smiled.
　4．The next program will start in July.
(5) Their smiles made me feel relaxed. の文型を答え、O と C を書きぬきなさい。

#### <span class="no">9</span>（条件英作文）次の対話は、留学生のエマ（Emma）とあなたの会話です。あとの問いに答えなさい。

(1) （　）に入る英文を、あとの条件にしたがって書きなさい。
Emma: I want to go to Kamakura this weekend, but this is my first time.
You: (　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　)

条件：① **know** と **how to** を使い、エマに「鎌倉への行き方を知っているか」をたずねる疑問文にすること。② 7語以上で書くこと。

(2) 次に、あなたがエマに「〜してほしい」と伝える文を、**want** を使って書きなさい（6語以上）。

## 解答・解説

<div class="kaito" markdown="1">

#### <span class="no">1</span>
(1) he lives　(2) this is　(3) wrote　(4) for　(5) to speak　(6) help　(7) to carry　(8) to make
**解説**　(1)(2) 間接疑問文は**ふつうの文の語順**。(3) who が主語の疑問文 Who wrote this letter? は、間接疑問文でもそのまま who wrote。(6) let のあとは原形。

#### <span class="no">2</span>
(1) I know what she wants.
(2) Do you know when his birthday is?
(3) Please tell me where you bought the bag.
(4) I don't know who broke the window.
(5) I wonder how many students there are in the gym.
**解説**　(1) does が消えて want → **wants**。(3) did が消えて buy → **bought**。(4) who が主語なので語順は変わらない。(5) how many students でひとかたまり。there are の語順にもどす。

#### <span class="no">3</span>
(1) It, to　(2) too, to　(3) what to　(4) asked, to　(5) let, use
**解説**　(2) too 〜 to には否定の意味がふくまれる。(5) 「使ってよい」と許したので let（過去形も let）。

#### <span class="no">4</span>
(1) I don't know where **the station is**.
(2) Do you know what time **he came**?
(3) I want him **to come** to the party.
(4) The movie made me **cry**.

#### <span class="no">5</span>
(1) Do you know who that woman is?　使わない語：**does**
(2) It is important for us to eat breakfast every morning.　使わない語：**of**
(3) Our teacher told us not to run in the classroom.　使わない語：**don't**
(4) I didn't know what to say.　使わない語：**how**
**解説**　(3) 「〜しないように」は not to。don't は使わない。

#### <span class="no">6</span>
(1) I don't know why he was late.
(2) I want you to visit my town.
(3) Can (Could / Will) you tell me how to use this computer?
(4) My mother made me cook (make) dinner.
**解説**　(1) Why was he late? → why he was late。(4) make＋O＋原形（SVOC）。

#### <span class="no">7</span>
(1) 1　(2) 2　(3) 1
**解説**　(2) visit は他動詞なので where to visit（どこを訪れればよいか）。go なら where to go。(3) help＋O＋原形。

#### <span class="no">8</span>
(1) (例) She asked him to read picture books to small children.
(2) 他の人の話を聞くことは大切だ（ということ）。
(3) Do you know when the next program starts?
(4) 3
(5) SVOC　O：me　C：feel (relaxed)
**解説**　(3) I didn't know what to say. は「疑問詞＋to」、ask the children what they thought も間接疑問文なので、ask the children what they thought を答えても正解。(4) 1 は「読み方がわからなかった」ので×。2 は「子どもたちに考えをたずねるように言った」ので×。4 は August なので×。(5) make＋O＋原形の SVOC。

#### <span class="no">9</span>
(1) (例) Do you know how to get to Kamakura?　／　Do you know how to go to Kamakura by train?
(2) (例) I want you to enjoy your trip to Kamakura.　／　I want you to take a lot of pictures there.
**解説**　(1) how to get to 〜 は「〜への行き方」の定番。Do you know で始まる疑問文にする。(2) want you to のあとは原形。語数を数えて条件を守ろう。

</div>
