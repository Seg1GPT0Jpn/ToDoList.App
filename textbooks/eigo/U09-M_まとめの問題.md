# 中2英語 Unit 9「不定詞と動名詞」まとめの問題

<p class="lead">Unit 9-1「不定詞の3用法」と Unit 9-2「動名詞と、不定詞との使い分け」の総まとめです。目標時間 45分。8割とれたら Unit 10 へ進みましょう。</p>

<div class="memo" markdown="1">

**チェックリスト**　□ to のあとは必ず動詞の原形　□ 名詞的用法は S・O・C の位置（消すと文がこわれる）
□ 形容詞的用法は名詞のすぐ後ろ（something to eat）　□ 副詞的用法は「目的」と「感情の原因」
□ enjoy / finish / stop＋ing、want / hope / decide＋to、like / start / begin はどちらも OK
□ 前置詞の後ろは ing　□ 動名詞の主語は単数あつかい　□ to＝これから、ing＝現実のイメージ

## まとめの問題

#### <span class="no">1</span> 次の英文の（　）内から最も適するものを選びなさい。

<div class="cols2" markdown="1">

(1) I want ( be ・ to be ・ being ) a vet.
(2) We enjoyed ( to swim ・ swimming ・ swim ) in the sea.
(3) Mika went to the station ( to meet ・ meeting ・ meet ) her friend.
(4) He is interested in ( to make ・ making ・ make ) robots.
(5) I have no time ( watch ・ to watch ・ watching ) TV.
(6) Did you decide ( to join ・ joining ・ join ) the tennis club?
(7) Please stop ( to talk ・ talking ・ talk ) in the library.
(8) ( Learn ・ Learning ・ Learns ) new words is important.

</div>

#### <span class="no">2</span> 下線部と同じ働きの語句をふくむ文を、1〜4の中から1つずつ選びなさい。

(1) My sister went to Osaka <u>to see</u> our grandmother.
　1．I want to see you.　2．He has a lot of things to see.　3．We got up early to see the sunrise.　4．To see is to believe.
(2) Do you have anything <u>to do</u> this afternoon?
　1．I have a book to read.　2．She tried to do her best.　3．I'm glad to do this job.　4．My plan is to do my homework.
(3) His job is <u>writing</u> stories for children.
　1．He was writing a letter then.　2．I finished writing the report.　3．Are you writing an e-mail?　4．She is writing in her notebook.
(4) <u>To play</u> the violin is not easy.
　1．I came here to play with you.　2．I want something to play with.　3．I was happy to play with my dog.　4．Ken began to play the violin.

#### <span class="no">3</span> 2つの文がほぼ同じ意味になるように、（　）に適する語を1語ずつ入れなさい。

(1) I like to sing songs.
　I like (　　) songs.
(2) Let's eat lunch under the tree.
　How (　　) (　　) lunch under the tree?
(3) I went to the shop. I wanted to buy a new pen.
　I went to the shop (　　) (　　) a new pen.
(4) Yuta can cook well.
　Yuta is good (　　) (　　).
(5) I have to do a lot of homework today.
　I have a lot of homework (　　) (　　) today.

#### <span class="no">4</span> 次の英文にはまちがいが1か所ずつあります。正しい英文に直しなさい。

<div class="cols2" markdown="1">

(1) I hope seeing you again.
(2) She finished to read the book.
(3) He wanted to bought a new bike.
(4) Collecting cards are my hobby.

</div>

#### <span class="no">5</span> 日本語の意味になるように、（　）内の語句を並べかえなさい。ただし、それぞれ**使わない語が1つ**あります。

(1) 彼らはその試合に勝ってとてもうれしかった。
　They ( very / happy / to / win / winning / were ) the game.
(2) 何か冷たい飲み物はありますか。
　Do you have ( cold / drink / anything / to / drinking )?
(3) 私の夢は世界中を旅することです。
　My dream ( is / traveling / travel / around ) the world.
(4) 私は英語で日記を書こうとした。
　I ( to / tried / write / writing / a diary ) in English.

#### <span class="no">7</span> 次の対話文の（　）に入る最も適するものを、1〜4の中から1つずつ選び、その番号を答えなさい。

(1) A: Why did you come to Japan, Ms. Green?
　B: (　　) Japanese culture.
　1．Study　2．To study　3．Studying　4．For study
(2) A: Thank you for (　　) me with my homework.
　B: You're welcome.
　1．help　2．to help　3．helping　4．helped

　B: Sorry. I need (　　) my room before dinner.

#### <span class="no">8</span> 次の英文は、中学2年生の Aoi が書いた英語のレポートです。これを読んで、あとの問いに答えなさい。

<div class="ex" markdown="1">

Last summer, I went to a small town in Nagano to stay with my aunt. She has a farm there. Every morning, I got up at five to help her. At first, getting up early was very hard for me. But I enjoyed picking tomatoes and corn with her.
One day, my aunt said, "Farmers work hard every day to grow good food for people." I was surprised to hear that. Before that summer, I didn't like vegetables, and I often stopped eating them at dinner. Now I try to eat everything.
In the future, I want to study about food at university. I hope to cook delicious food for many people someday.

</div>

(1) Aoi はなぜ毎朝5時に起きたのですか。日本語で答えなさい。
(2) 本文中の **getting** と同じ働きの ing 形を、本文中から2つさがして抜き出しなさい。（ただし getting 以外）
(3) 次の質問に英語で答えなさい。
　① Did Aoi enjoy picking tomatoes on the farm?
　② What does Aoi want to study at university?
(4) 本文の内容に合うものとして最も適するものを、1〜4の中から1つ選び、その番号を答えなさい。
　1．Aoi's aunt went to Nagano to help Aoi.
　2．Getting up early was easy for Aoi at first.
　3．Aoi was surprised to hear her aunt's words about farmers.
　4．Aoi stopped eating vegetables after the summer.

#### <span class="no">9</span>（条件英作文）あなたのクラスに来る留学生に、あなたの「好きなこと」と「将来の夢」を伝えます。次の条件にしたがって英語3文で書きなさい。

- 1文目は**動名詞**を使って、好きなこと（趣味）を書く。
- 2文目は**名詞的用法の不定詞**を使って、将来の夢を書く。
- 3文目は**副詞的用法（目的）の不定詞**を使って、その夢のために今していることを書く。
- それぞれ5語以上の英文にすること。

## 解答・解説

<div class="kaito" markdown="1">

#### <span class="no">1</span>
(1) to be　(2) swimming　(3) to meet　(4) making　(5) to watch　(6) to join　(7) talking　(8) Learning
**解説**　(4) in は前置詞 → ing。(5) time を説明する形容詞的用法。(7) stop to talk だと「話すために立ち止まる」になる。(8) 動名詞の主語。V が is であることもヒント。

#### <span class="no">2</span>
(1) 3　(2) 1　(3) 2　(4) 4
**解説**　(1) 目的の副詞的用法。(2) anything を説明する形容詞的用法。1 も a book を説明。(3) His job ＝ writing（動名詞）。2 は finished の O の動名詞。1・3・4 は進行形。(4) 文の S＝名詞的用法。4 は began の O。

#### <span class="no">3</span>
(1) singing　(2) about, eating（having）　(3) to, buy　(4) at, cooking　(5) to, do
**解説**　(5) have to do 〜（〜しなければならない）を、homework＋to do（やるべき宿題）に書きかえる。have の意味が「持っている」に変わる。

#### <span class="no">4</span>
(1) I hope **to see** you again.
(2) She finished **reading** the book.
(3) He wanted to **buy** a new bike.
(4) Collecting cards **is** my hobby.

#### <span class="no">5</span>
(1) They were very happy to win the game.　使わない語：**winning**
(2) Do you have anything cold to drink?　使わない語：**drinking**
(3) My dream is traveling around the world.　使わない語：**travel**
(4) I tried to write a diary in English.　使わない語：**writing**
**解説**　(2) 〈anything＋形容詞＋to 〜〉の順。(3) to がないので、is のあとの C には動名詞を使う。

#### <span class="no">6</span>
(1) My brother wants to be (become) a pilot.
(2) We enjoyed practicing the guitar yesterday.
(3) I went to Yuki's house to do my homework (with her).
(4) It started (began) to rain. ／ It started (began) raining.
**解説**　(1) 主語が3人称単数なので wants。to のあとは原形 be。(2) enjoy＋ing。(3) to do は目的。(4) start / begin はどちらも OK。

#### <span class="no">7</span>
(1) 2　(2) 3　(3) 1
**解説**　(1) Why に「〜するため」と答える To 〜。(2) for は前置詞 → ing。(3) need は to だけ。

#### <span class="no">8</span>
(1) おばさん（の農場の仕事）を手伝うため。
(2) picking ／ eating（順不同）
(3) ① Yes, she did.　② She wants to study about food.
(4) 3
**解説**　(1) I got up at five **to help her**（目的）。(2) getting は「早く起きること」という文の主語＝動名詞。picking は enjoyed の O、eating は stopped の O。(4) I was surprised to hear that. の that は、おばさんの言葉。4 は逆で、今は何でも食べようとしている。

#### <span class="no">9</span>
（解答例）I like playing the piano very much. ／ My dream is to be a music teacher. ／ I practice the piano every day to be a good pianist.
**解説**　① 動名詞（〜ing）、② My dream is to 〜（C の位置）、③ 文の最後に「to＋原形（〜するために）」、④ 各5語以上。3つの文で**不定詞・動名詞の位置**がちがうことを意識できると満点。
